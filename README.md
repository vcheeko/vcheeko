## Building now — KORAKO YOLAWANI

**A human-directed operating layer for reliable AI-assisted work across models, tools, devices and long-running projects.**

Korako is being built to reduce the amount of manual coordination a person must do between AI systems: carrying context, deciding what happens next, moving work between tools, checking whether execution actually happened and recovering when a chain breaks.

> **Goal → Plan → Authority → Execution → Evidence → Verification → Continuation**

### Why the name — Korako Yolawani

The name is part of the product idea, not just a label.

**Korako** comes from the Slovenian idea of **korak / koraki — a step / steps**. The product is built around helping a person move through complex work one meaningful step at a time, while preserving context, direction and control.

**Yolawani** represents the wider **human journey around those steps** — the life, goals, decisions, projects and experiences that give each step meaning. It is used as the distinctive second part of the brand rather than as a literal translation from another language.

There is also a philosophical parallel I like in Japanese:

- **道 (*michi*)** — path / way;
- **人生 (*jinsei*)** — human life / one's life;
- **人生の道 (*jinsei no michi*)** — *the path of life* / *the way through life*.

This Japanese expression is **inspiration and a conceptual parallel, not the linguistic origin or literal translation of Korako Yolawani**.

The product idea behind the name is simple:

> **A life is not one task. It is a journey made of steps. Korako helps the human move through those steps without losing the journey.**

**KORA** is the private orchestration core. **Mira** is the conversational interface. **Korako Yolawani** is the product and the human journey around both.

## How I build

- **Human-directed:** consequential decisions remain human-owned.
- **Evidence-first:** a prepared action is not treated as executed, and execution is not treated as verified without evidence.
- **AI-native:** AI is used heavily for research, implementation assistance, critique, documentation and testing.
- **Multi-tool:** models and tools are treated as capabilities that can be routed rather than as the identity of the product.
- **Recovery-aware:** permissions, reversibility, checkpoints and failure recovery are designed in rather than added later.

My focus is the intersection of **product vision, systems decomposition, AI-native workflows, acceptance criteria, prototyping, testing direction and orchestration**.

## Public / private boundary

The public Korako GitHub is intentionally a **diligence surface, not a source dump**.

**Public:** product thesis, high-level architecture, sanitized evidence contracts, reproducibility targets, limitations and review protocol.  
**Private:** canonical runtime, credentials, machine configuration, security-sensitive boundaries, operational logs and unpublished IP-sensitive material.

Security issues should be reported privately rather than opened as public vulnerability issues; see the flagship repository security policy.
