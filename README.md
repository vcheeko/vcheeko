## What I'm building

### KORA

A human-directed operating layer for reliable AI-assisted work. KORA explores
durable project state, dependencies, risk-proportional authority, bounded
execution, verification, recovery and continuation across models and tools.

**Current stage:** prototype and evidence-building - not production-ready.

### TRI-GLAV

An outdoor-safety platform concept focused on helping people explore, grow and
return home safely. The product vision includes offline navigation, safety
information, hazards, check-ins and emergency support.

[View the TRI-GLAV project](https://vcheeko.github.io/tri-glav-website/)

### The First Second

A 3D simulation-game concept built around one question: can we prove that our
reality is not a simulation?

[View the repository](https://github.com/vcheeko/the-first-second)
