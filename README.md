## One focus: Korako Yolawani

I am building **Korako Yolawani**, a human-first AI companion designed to help people handle important work with less administration and more control.

The idea is simple: people should be able to explain what they want to achieve, understand the next step, approve important actions, and see what was actually completed.

**Mira** is the planned conversational front door. Korako's underlying system is designed to coordinate tools and workflows while preserving human authority.

### Korako People and Korako Business

**One product, two connected areas:** Korako People supports job search, applications and interview preparation; Korako Business supports customer conversations, meetings and follow-through. These are product directions in development, not two complete services.

**First business hypothesis:** small US residential HVAC companies, helping office teams understand which customer enquiries remain unresolved, what should happen next and who is responsible.

**Immediate priority:** a working, independently verified core and one complete workflow before inviting others to try it. Customer demand and business results still need to be tested.

### Korako People: finding work

We also want to help **job seekers** move through a complete, human-controlled career journey:

- discover and assess relevant job opportunities;
- build a truthful master CV and tailor it to each role;
- prepare applications and cover letters for review;
- practice interviews and receive constructive feedback;
- track applications, replies and next steps.

These are **planned and experimental product capabilities**, not a claim of a complete public job-search service. Applications or messages should not be sent without the person's approval.

### Principles

- **Human in control:** consequential actions require appropriate authorization.
- **Evidence before claims:** prepared, executed and verified are distinct.
- **Transparent progress:** show what happened and what remains unresolved.
- **Privacy and cost awareness:** favor appropriate local, included or lower-cost tools.
- **One journey:** connect useful steps without losing the person's intent.
